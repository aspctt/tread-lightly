<p>Tread Lightly gives every block its own footsteps. Vanilla picks one sound per block and plays it at a fixed rate. Tread Lightly works out what is actually under each foot, in turn, and plays something appropriate for how you are moving over it.</p>

<p>It replaces the sounds, not the game. Nothing is added, nothing is craftable, no block or entity behaves differently, and no world data changes.</p>

<h3>Sound packs</h3>

<p>What a block sounds like is defined by a pack, not by the mod. Block maps, acoustics, and audio all come from resource packs, read through the ordinary resource pack system, so packs stack, reload with F3+T, and override each other in the order the player sets.</p>

<p>Adding a sound to a block requires no code and no mod update.</p>

<h3>Client only</h3>

<p>The mod runs entirely on the machine that renders the world. A dedicated server neither loads it nor needs to know it exists, and two players in the same world can run different packs and legitimately hear different things.</p>

<h3>Simplified</h3>

<p>This is a deliberately smaller take on the idea than the mod it comes from: a leaner engine and a format meant to be legible to someone who has never seen it before.</p>

<h3>Requirements</h3>

<p>Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge 21.1.235 or newer. Client side only.</p>
